Blackline

Blackline features satisfying gun and sword play and fast paced action, inspired by 90's shooters.

OVERVIEW

Slide and move around like a maniac, shoot Androids and decapitate them. Very old-school boomer shooter inspired run 'n' gun action.

Destroy the enemies in your path, and find the exit!

MY ROLE

  • Character & weapon art.
  • NPC programming.
  • First person animations.
  • Scalable and editable weapons system.
  • Player movement system.

RESULTS & LEARNINGS

The project was done in a great hurry, as it was for a course. We had roughly 12 weeks to complete it. Luckily, my teammate, Veeti Paasonen, was more than competent enough to handle the tempo. We managed to complete Blackline by the deadline. Unfortunately, some enemies and weapons were cut.

Time management is underrated. Being honest with the project scope given the time to complete it is crucial. It was something I was not personally critical of. Definitely an important lesson.
